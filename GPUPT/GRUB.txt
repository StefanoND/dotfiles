These are the parameters that should be in your grub, in this order. Numbers 6 and 7 are OPTIONAL and UNSAFE

0. Prevents nouveau from initializing before vfio-pci binds to avoid conflicts
"nouveau.modeset=0"

1. Power Saving, CPU Power and Performance Settings
"cpufreq.default_governor=performance"

2. ACPI and Power Management
"initcall_blacklist=acpi_cpufreq_init acpi_enforce_resources=lax"

3. AMP P-State driver config
"amd_pstate.shared_mem=1 amd_pstate=active"

4. Enabling AVIC (must disable nested virtualization else AVIC won't work)
"kvm_amd.avic=1 kvm_amd.force_avic=1 kvm_amd.nested=0 kvm_amd.sev=0 kvm_amd.npt=1"

5. IOMMU for proper PCI passthrough
"amd_iommu=on iommu=pt"

6. PCIe Overrides, Forces PCIe ACS separation for "better" passthrough (DON'T USE IF YOU'RE NOT HAVING PROBLEMS)
"pcie_acs_override=downstream,multifunction"

7. Fixing "problems" with interrupt remapping support (DON'T USE IF YOU'RE NOT HAVING PROBLEMS)
"vfio_iommu_type1.allow_unsafe_interrupts=1"

8. GPU-Passthrough
"vfio-pci.ids=xxxx:xxxx,xxxx:xxxx rd.driver.pre=vfio-pci video=vesafb:off,efifb:off"

9. Windows, Controls ignores MSRs
"kvm.ignore_msrs=1 kvm.report_ignored_msrs=0"

10. cgroup hierarchy for better container and resources management
"systemd.unified_cgroup_hierarchy=1"
